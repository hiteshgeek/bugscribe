## 🎯 Technical Considerations

### Chat Platform

- **Backend**: Node.js + Socket.io or Elixir + Phoenix Channels
- **Database**: PostgreSQL + Redis (caching)
- **Real-time**: WebSockets
- **Storage**: AWS S3 or Cloudflare R2
- **CDN**: Cloudflare or AWS CloudFront
- **Search**: Elasticsearch or Algolia

### Video Platform

- **WebRTC**: Mediasoup or Janus Gateway
- **SFU/MCU**: Self-hosted or Twilio/Agora
- **Recording**: FFmpeg
- **Transcoding**: AWS MediaConvert
- **Streaming**: HLS or WebRTC

## 📈 Go-to-Market Strategy

### Product 1 (Documentation Tool)

- **Primary Market**: SaaS companies, dev tool companies
- **Marketing**: SEO (how-to guides), Product Hunt, dev communities
- **Sales Motion**: Self-serve → sales for enterprise

### Product 2 (Changelog Platform)

- **Primary Market**: SaaS products, mobile apps
- **Marketing**: Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, Twitter
- **Sales Motion**: 100% self-serve, land & expand

### Product 3 (Chat/Video)

- **Primary Market**: SMBs tired of Slack/Zoom prices
- **Marketing**: Reddit ads, comparison pages, affiliate program
- **Sales Motion**: Freemium → upgrade based on team size

---

## 🚨 Risk Assessment

### Product 1 - Medium Risk

- **Pros**: Clear use case, people pay for documentation tools
- **Cons**: Competitive market, but niche enough to win

### Product 2 - Low Risk

- **Pros**: Easier to build, clear monetization, fast time-to-market
- **Cons**: Smaller TAM, but great bootstrapping opportunity

### Product 3 - High Risk

- **Pros**: Huge market, recurring revenue
- **Cons**: Very expensive to build & scale, dominated by giants

---

## 💡 Recommendation

**Build them in this order:**

1. **Changelog Platform** (easiest, fastest revenue)
2. **Documentation Tool** (leverage learnings from #1)
3. **Chat/Video** (only if you have funding or team)

**Or focus entirely on one and make it exceptional.**
